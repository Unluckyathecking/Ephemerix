    About this map

    This is a star chart drawn from a catalogue, not SPHEREx images. The real images are one tap away: open any marker, or drop a pin anywhere and we build the view from SPHEREx and WISE data in about a minute.

    The orange rings are views we have already built; the cyan diamonds are well-known nearby objects we can build live. Zoom right in on a fast mover and an arrow shows its path from 2010 to 2026, from its catalogue motion. Star colours follow each star’s measured colour; dashed lines show the ecliptic (where the planets travel) and the plane of the Milky Way.

    Stars: the Hipparcos Catalogue (ESA 1997), from the VizieR service, positions moved to the year 2000. Constellation lines and star names: Stellarium’s “modern” sky culture, CC BY-SA 4.0.
